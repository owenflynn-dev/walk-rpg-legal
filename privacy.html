Privacy Policy

App: Walk RPG: Stepslayer · Last updated: 8 September 2026 (friends added)

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Walk RPG ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

Summary

Walk RPG has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your steps, your walk routes and your whole game save stay on your device. The only features that send anything to a server are the optional Raid Party and Friends features, described below, and you have to switch them on yourself.

Health and Fitness Data (Steps)

With your permission, the App reads your daily step count from Apple Health (HealthKit) on iOS and from Health Connect on Android. Steps are the game: each step becomes one point of damage against that week's boss.

This is a read-only permission. The App never writes to Apple Health or Health Connect, and reads nothing else — no heart rate, no workouts, no weight, no sleep, no medical records.

Your step data is processed entirely on your device and stored only in the App's local storage. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else, is not used for advertising, and is not sold or shared. You can revoke this permission at any time in your device's Health or Health Connect settings; the App keeps working and simply stops counting new steps.

Location Data (Walk Mode)

Walk Mode is an optional feature that records a single walk: its route, distance, pace and splits. While it is running — and only while it is running — the App uses your device's GPS, including in the background if you allow it, so the recording continues with your screen off.

Recorded routes are stored on your device and never uploaded. When you share a walk, the App draws an abstract pixel trail; it never includes map imagery, coordinates or addresses.

The map shown during a walk uses map tiles from OpenStreetMap. Requesting a map tile necessarily reveals your device's IP address and the area being viewed to that service; see the OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy. Your recorded route itself is never sent.

You can decline location access entirely. Everything except Walk Mode continues to work.

Your Game Save

Your hero, level, weekly history, badges, wardrobe and walk log are stored locally on your device. Deleting the App deletes them. The App can write a backup file to your device's Files storage, and you can export a backup yourself — those files stay wherever you put them, and we never receive them.

Friends & Raid Parties (Optional)

A Raid Party lets a few friends pool their weekly step totals into one shared bar. It is off by default and only starts when you create or join a party.

If you use it, the following is sent to our backend, hosted by Supabase on servers in the United States:

  • the display name you type in (choose anything; it does not have to be your real name),
  • your step total for the current week and your weekly goal,
  • the party's code and which week it belongs to.

Your route data, walk history, badges and game save are never sent. Party records are scoped to a single week.

If you use the Friends tab you also choose a username, which is stored with your account and shown to the friends you add; we store your friend list, an opaque invite link token, and your lifetime step total, so friends can rank each other on an all-time leaderboard. Friends see your username, this week's steps and your lifetime steps — nobody else does. There is no public profile, no search, no chat. You can remove or block a friend at any time; blocking is permanent and mutual. Step counts are reported by each player's own phone and are not verified.

By default you join anonymously — no email, no name, no identifier from you. You may optionally sign in with Apple or Google so a party follows you to a new phone. Signing in tells us only the account identifier the provider returns; it does not back up or sync your game save, and the App says so at the point of sign-in. See the Apple Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can delete your account and every party, friend and leaderboard record associated with it at any time from Hero → Raid Party → Delete Account. Your hero and walks live on your phone and are unaffected.

What We Do Not Do

The App contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, and no third-party tracking. We do not sell or share personal information, and nothing in the App is used for behavioural advertising.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will remove it.

Your Rights

Because almost everything is local, you control it directly: revoke Health or location permission in device settings, reset all data from the App's Settings screen, or delete the App. For anything held on our backend, the in-app account deletion above removes it; you can also email us and we will delete it on request.
