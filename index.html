Walk RPG: Stepslayer — Support

Walk RPG: Stepslayer turns your real daily steps into damage against a weekly boss. Walk, open the app, and the steps you took since you last played are dealt as damage. There are no accounts for the game itself — your hero lives on your phone. Support is by email: questions, bug reports and account deletion requests all go to owenflynn2093@gmail.com, and we aim to reply within a few days.
